# Slang AI

> Slang AI is a voice-AI phone-answering and reservation platform for restaurants. Its virtual phone agent answers every inbound call 24/7, responds to guest inquiries in a natural human-like voice, and automatically manages reservations, with official integrations to OpenTable, SevenRooms, Tripleseat, Yelp, and Fishbowl. Founded by former Spotify data scientists, it reports 96%+ guest satisfaction (CSAT) and 50% more phone reservations for its restaurant customers.

This llms.txt was generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from the public Slang AI marketing surface. Slang AI is a hosted SaaS product with no public developer API, OpenAPI specification, SDKs, or developer portal at the time of writing.
